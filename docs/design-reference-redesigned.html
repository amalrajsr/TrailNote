Privacy

Privacy at TrailNote

How TrailNote handles the information you share while keeping public traveler knowledge useful.

Last updated September 2026

What we collect

TrailNote stores account information needed for Google sign-in, traveler tips you publish, and operational records needed to keep the service reliable and safe.

What appears publicly

Public contribution pages show the display name you use on TrailNote, the practical details you choose to share, the visit month when provided, and whether a public service contact is available.

Your sign-in email and authentication details are not shown on public traveler pages.

Public service contacts

Phone numbers are stored separately from public contribution data. A reader must deliberately choose to reveal an available public business or service number before it is returned.

Photos

Uploaded traveler photos are processed before storage. TrailNote is designed to remove unnecessary metadata such as location metadata from accepted images before they are attached to public tips.

Operational data

Operational logs may record request timing, error codes and service diagnostics. TrailNote should not intentionally log tip bodies, phone numbers, email addresses, image buffers, OAuth tokens or secrets.

Your choices

Authors can edit or delete their own contributions. Public service contacts can be reported for review without requiring the affected person to create an account.

Contact and removal

If you believe a public business or service number should not appear on TrailNote, use the contact-removal flow linked from the relevant contribution so the exact number can be identified safely.

Terms

Terms for using TrailNote

TrailNote is a community-maintained collection of practical travel information. These terms explain the basic expectations for reading and contributing.

Last updated September 2026

Use traveler information with judgment

Prices, routes, opening conditions and availability can change. TrailNote presents what travelers report from their own experiences; it does not guarantee that a contribution remains current.

Share your own experience

Contributions should be based on something you actually experienced or observed. Do not copy reviews, descriptions or photos from another person or website.

Respect people and privacy

Do not publish private personal information. Public-facing business or service contact details should only be shared when you are comfortable making that information available to other travelers.

Moderation

TrailNote may hide contributions or contacts that are spam, unsafe, inaccurate, abusive or privacy-invasive. Moderation actions should be recorded internally and handled consistently.

Your content

You remain responsible for the information you contribute. You may edit or delete your own published traveler tips subject to the product's revision and moderation rules.
