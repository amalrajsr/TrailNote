Transport Ooty · Tamil Nadu

Take the local bus instead of an auto from the railway station

Local busAbout 25 min

Walk a little towards the main road and catch the town bus. Autos quote much more to tourists, while the bus is frequent during the day.

Visited Aug 2026 Recently verified
Stay Hampi · Karnataka

Guest houses across the river can be much cheaper on weekdays

₹700 / night

Private roomWalk-in

If you are flexible about the exact location, ask two or three guest houses before booking. Prices varied a lot for similar rooms.

Visited Jul 2026 Recently verified
Food Manali · Himachal Pradesh

Eat a little away from Mall Road for simpler, cheaper meals

Local cafés10 min walk

The lanes towards Old Manali have several small places where a normal meal costs noticeably less than the restaurants right on Mall Road.

Visited Jun 2026 Check before going
Quick tip Badami · Karnataka

Start the cave temples early if you want time for the fort too

Morning3–4 hours

The caves themselves do not take the entire day, but the heat builds quickly. Starting early makes it much easier to include the fort and lake.

Visited May 2026 Recently verified
