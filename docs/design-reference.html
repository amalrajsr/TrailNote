Badami → Pattadakal

₹35 / person / trip

Local bus · About 40 min

The local bus was an easy way to get to Pattadakal. I paid ₹35 from the main bus stand. Autos quoted much more, and buses were frequent during the day.

Ask the conductor for the stop near the temples. Keep some change for the fare.
