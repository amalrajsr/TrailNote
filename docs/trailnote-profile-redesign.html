Transport

Agree on the auto fare before you get in

Auto fares around the tourist areas can be higher than expected. Agree on the price before the ride, especially for short trips from the bus stand or railway station.

Stay on the Hampi Bazaar side if you rely on buses

It makes the early-morning temple areas and the bus connection easier to manage without arranging a separate ride.
